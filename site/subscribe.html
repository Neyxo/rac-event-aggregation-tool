Subscribe to a calendar

A subscription puts the events into the calendar app you already use and keeps them up to date by itself: new events appear, moved ones move, cancelled ones are marked. Nothing to install. Members-only events are not included; registration still happens on Polaris.

iPhone / iPad (Apple Calendar)
  1. Tap Apple / Outlook next to a calendar below.
  2. Confirm "Subscribe" in the dialog that opens.
  3. Done. It updates by itself (Settings › Calendar › Accounts lets you change how often).
Google Calendar
  1. Tap Google next to a calendar below. On a phone this opens the Google Calendar website; sign in if asked.
  2. Confirm "Add calendar".
  3. In the Google Calendar app, open Settings and make sure the new calendar is switched on. Google refreshes subscribed calendars every few hours up to a day.
Android (Samsung or other calendar apps)
  1. Use the Google button: most Android calendar apps show your Google calendars.
  2. Then enable the new calendar in your calendar app's settings.
Outlook / Windows / Mac
  1. Tap Apple / Outlook. On a Mac this opens Calendar; in Outlook choose "Add calendar › Subscribe from web".
  2. If nothing opens, copy the .ics link and paste it into "Add calendar from URL" in your calendar app.
